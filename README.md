# Bull Run: Escape the Bear

A humorous 3D endless runner with a stock market theme. A retail trader runs down Dalal Street with a bear chasing behind.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. The game is a single file; it loads three.js from a CDN, so an internet connection is needed.

## Controls

- Left / Right arrows (or A / D): switch lane
- Up arrow, W or Space: jump
- Down arrow or S: slide
- P or Esc: pause
- Phone: swipe left, right, up, down

## What's in it

- Obstacles: Brokerage, STT, GST, Hidden Charges (jump); Tough Times, Margin Call, Market Crash (slide); Loss candles, EMI, Hot Tip From Uncle, Corporate Job (dodge)
- Power-ups: Bull Run (invulnerable, smashes everything), Stop Loss (absorbs one hit), Dividend Magnet, Bonus Issue (2x)
- Speed rises with coins collected
- Original looping music, bear and bull roars, and separate music / effects volume settings
- Joke news ticker, billboards and game-over lines
